Micron: The Guide Mattered More Than the Quarter

What Korea’s memory stocks took from the U.S. print

BOTTOM LINEMicron beat estimates for the quarter just reported and guided next-quarter revenue nearly 8% above consensus. For Samsung Electronics and SK hynix, it is another read on the same memory cycle.

Micron · next-quarter revenue guide
USD 61.5bn
LSEG consensus USD 57.02bn · midpoint +7.9%

The Next-Quarter Guide Was the Bigger Signal

WHAT · Fiscal Q4 results

Revenue USD 54.23bn · adjusted EPS USD 33.42 · gross margin 87.0% · above both LSEG consensus (USD 51.07bn · USD 31.61) and company guidance (USD 50.0bn ±1.0bn · USD 31 ±1) · previous-quarter revenue USD 41.46bn

GUIDE · Next-quarter outlook

Revenue USD 61.5bn (±1.5bn) · midpoint 7.9% above LSEG consensus of USD 57.02bn · adjusted EPS USD 38.15 (±1.00), 7.8% above consensus of USD 35.40 · gross margin approximately 86.25%

VISIBILITY · Why this may last beyond one quarter

Micron expects memory and storage supply-demand conditions in 2027–2028 to be much tighter than in 2026 · 26 strategic customer agreements · covering more than 35% of expected revenue through 2030 · USD 32bn in customer financial commitments, mostly cash deposits

HBM · 2027 volume and pricing

By Micron’s own figures, most of its 2027 HBM supply volume is already contracted · pricing is materially higher year on year, narrowing the margin gap with conventional DRAM · these figures are separate from Samsung Electronics’ and SK hynix’s contract pricing

KOREA · What it means for Korean memory (interpretation)

The signal extends beyond HBM: data-center demand remains firm across server memory more broadly · that points in the same direction as Korea’s September chip exports, where both volumes and contract prices rose

BUT · Why didn’t Micron shares jump?

Both results and next-quarter guidance beat estimates, yet reports said the initial after-hours reaction was muted · next-quarter gross-margin guidance of about 86.25% is slightly below the 87.0% just reported · Micron said performance-based compensation costs embedded in Q4 inventory will flow through next quarter’s margins · another interpretation is that expectations were already elevated after the stock’s strong run this year
