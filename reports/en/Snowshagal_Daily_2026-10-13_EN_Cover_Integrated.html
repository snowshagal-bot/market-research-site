WHY / 02

China's DRAM Plans, AI Funding Questions and Fading Domestic Support

What drove today's decline

IN BRIEFU.S. memory stocks sold off first on a Chinese rival's server-DRAM plans, with financing concerns around AI investment adding to the pressure. In Korea, thinner buy-side support allowed losses to deepen after the open.

01

The Main Catalyst · CXMT Plans to Launch Server DRAM This Year

What was announced · ChangXin Memory Technologies (CXMT) president Cao Kanyu introduced a DRAM architecture at an integrated-circuits and chiplet conference in Shanghai on Oct. 10. He said the company plans to bring a server DRAM product (DDR5 RDIMM) using the design to market by year-end · International media reported the announcement on Oct. 12Why the market cared · This was a plan for server DDR5, not proof of competitiveness in high-bandwidth memory (HBM). Still, greater competition in conventional server DRAM could affect prices and margins (interpretation). Samsung Electronics, SK hynix and Micron have dominated server DRAM, while CXMT has focused mainly on PCs, smartphones and consumer electronicsProduct launch is not volume production · CXMT has outlined a year-end launch of server DDR5 using 4F². Samsung Electronics and SK hynix are also developing related technology but have announced no firm commercialization timetable. CXMT's actual yields, supply capacity and server-customer qualification remain unverified; the design may initially appear in only some products. Kiwoom Securities' Han Ji-young argues it is premature to price in Chinese oversupply that has not yet materializedMarket reaction · Oct. 12 U.S. session: Micron -3.88% · SK hynix -4.02% · -2.67%

A 4F² cell occupies about 33% less area than today's mainstream 6F² design. CXMT says its proprietary vertical-gate transistor reduces leakage. On Sept. 20, it also announced volume production of its fifth-generation (G5) DRAM platform and a 24Gb LPDDR5X product
Reports have suggested around 2028 for Samsung Electronics' 4F² volume production, but the company has not confirmed that timetable
TrendForce cautions that a commercial launch does not mean a product is ready for high-volume manufacturing. Server DRAM requires reliability under continuous operation and compatibility testing with third-party CPUs and motherboards
Competing views · Counterpoint's Neil Shah says the market could look very different in a year if CXMT expands capacity. At Micron's Sept. 30 earnings call, its CEO projected demand would continue to exceed supply in 2027 and 2028

02

A Second Pressure Point · Who Will Finance the AI Buildout?

At a DBS event panel in Singapore on Oct. 12, Broadcom CEO Hock Tan compared some investors' extreme expectations to casino gambling, saying many did not know what they were investing in. He was criticizing investor behavior, not dismissing AI as an industry. He also argued that capital, rather than shortages of chips or memory, may become the next constraint on AI expansionIntel CEO Lip-Bu Tan stressed that investors need to see returns to maintain their interest. He identified memory as one of the areas where growth could slow first, a warning with a particularly direct bearing on Korea's memory heavyweights (interpretation)Neither CEO dismissed strong computing demand, however. Lip-Bu Tan expects hardware supply constraints to last another two to three years; Hock Tan expects demand to exceed supply through 2029–2030 · Market reaction: Broadcom -1.88% · Intel -3.20% · Nvidia -1.29%

What Amplified the Selloff in Korea · More Selling, Less Support
