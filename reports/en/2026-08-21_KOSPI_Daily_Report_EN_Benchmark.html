One-Line Glossary
sidecar
If KOSPI 200 futures move at least 5% from the reference price for one minute, or if KOSDAQ 150 futures move at least 6% while the cash index moves at least 3% for one minute, program-trading orders are suspended for five minutes· Triggered on the sell side in KOSDAQ at 10:05 on Aug. 21
Limit Up· Limit Down
Daily price limit ±30%· Aug. 21 KOSPI limit-up 0· KOSDAQ limit-up 5· KOSDAQ limit-down 3
Treasury buybacks
A Treasury debt-management tool that repurchases outstanding securities· The Aug. 19 expansion announcement was intended to support liquidity in 10–30Y maturities· Different from reducing net debt or Federal Reserve QE
CMT
Official Treasury maturity yields based on 15:30 ET quote collection· Not the actual final transaction price
SOX
Philadelphia Semiconductor Index· Representative gauge of global semiconductor stocks
S7
A theme grouping seven large-cap semiconductor stocks including Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix
HBM
High Bandwidth Memory· DRAM stacked in multiple layers to increase bandwidth
Materials, Parts & Equipment
materials · parts · equipment· suppliers of materials and equipment to major semiconductor producers
Producer Prices
Output-price index for goods and services supplied domestically by companies· Compiled by the Bank of Korea· Feeds into consumer prices with a lag
EPS
Earnings Per Share· Net income ÷ shares outstanding· Adjusted figures exclude one-off gains/losses
Consensus· Guidance
Consensus = average of brokerage forecasts· Guidance = outlook issued by the company
Short Selling
Selling borrowed shares → a bearish position· Aug. 21 KOSPI KRW2.16tn· 5.4% of combined KRX+NXT turnover· Final regular session
Flow Concentration
Share of each side's aggregate stock-level net buys/sells accounted for by the top names· KRX data
Program Trading
Automated multi-stock orders· A channel through which indexes and investor flows move· Included in investor flows
Arbitrage· Non-arbitrage
Arbitrage = exploits cash–futures price differences· Non-arbitrage = index-tracking and other general program trading
Cash–Futures Basis
Cash closes 15:30 / futures 15:45 → not the same timestamp, so excluded from directional signals· Positive basis = contango
USD/KRWDaytime-Session Close
15:30 close of the domestic FX regular daytime session· Different from the interbank Market Average Rate series· Reference USD/KRWvalue used in the report
Base Exchange Rate
Volume-weighted average of interbank FX transactions· Market-average-rate series· Used only for JPY/KRWper ¥100
Sector· Theme Performance
Sector = market-cap weighted· Theme = simple average of constituents· When values conflict, sector data take precedence
Market-cap weighted
Sector indexes weight constituents by market capitalization· larger stocks have more influence
Market Breadth
Advancer ratio· Measures breadth across advancing and declining stocks
