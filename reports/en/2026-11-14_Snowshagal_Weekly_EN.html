LEGISLATIVE CATALYST

Power grids and cablesAI Power Infrastructure +9.09%

This week

Prior week: -12.53% ; roughly 60% of the prior decline recovered. The Senate advanced only procedural votes: 73–25 on cloture on November 9 U.S. time, then 62–21 to proceed on November 10.

Why it matters

The bill would require data centers to share grid costs and could shorten transmission permitting from six to ten years to about two (DS Investment & Securities). Hanwha Investment & Securities favors cables as the top play, with power equipment and cables showing the sharpest upside.

The estimate

Assuming passage, DS Investment & Securities forecasts U.S. transmission-grid demand to rise next year by +50% .

Next steps

After a judicial confirmation vote at or after 07:30 KST on November 17 (November 16 U.S. afternoon), another cloture vote on a substitute amendment is expected. Final Senate passage and House action would still be required. Watch U.S. power-equipment contract disclosures.

Risk

The bill fails or stalls. Hanwha notes that new data-center rules may not take effect until January next year at the earliest.
