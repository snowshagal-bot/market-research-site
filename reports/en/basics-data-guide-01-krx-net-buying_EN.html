Short on time?Three key takeaways
  • Log in to the Korea Exchange's KRX Data Marketplace (data.krx.co.kr) and open Statistics → Basic statistics → Stocks → Trading → Top net purchases by investor, screen [12010]. The investor type starts out set to 'Financial investment'.
  • Despite the name, the table lists every stock that investor group traded, ranked by net purchases. The biggest sales are at the very bottom.
  • Regular-session data appears around 3:45 p.m.; final figures that include after-hours trading arrive around 8 p.m. The same query can return different numbers depending on when you run it.

01The route

Finding the Screen

"What did foreign investors buy today?" You can check before the news stories come out, on a Korea Exchange (KRX) statistics screen. These are official figures compiled by the exchange itself.

Screen name투자자별 순매수상위종목 (Top net purchases by investor)
Screen number[12010]
Internal codeMDCSTAT024. The data request itself is called MDCSTAT02401. Korean news stories and research reports often cite the screen by this code.

Four clicks

You can also type 12010 into the screen number/name search box at the top left. The same Trading menu holds two related screens: 투자자별 거래실적 (trading by investor), which shows market-wide totals, and 투자자별 거래실적(개별종목) (trading by investor, individual stock), which follows one stock day by day.

As of November 2026, the statistics screens open only after you sign up and log in. The site, formerly called the KRX Market Data System (정보데이터시스템), is now KRX Data Marketplace. The address is still data.krx.co.kr.

(sell, buy, net) and three for trading valueThe won amount traded. The ranking is based on net purchases by value. (sell, buy, net). Net purchasesPurchases minus sales. Warm-upA real query

For November 16, you switch the investor type to 'foreigners' and search all markets. The top row is SK Square, with net purchases of ₩27.4 billion. How did foreign investors trade overall that day?

The answer is ②

Scroll to the end of the table and the last three rows are Samsung Electronics preferred shares at −₩108.7 billion, SK hynix at −₩204.2 billion and Samsung Electronics at −₩621.0 billion. Only 803 of the 2,629 stocks showed net purchases, and the net purchases add up to about −₩1.536 trillion (KOSPI, KOSDAQ and KONEX combined, final figures including after-hours trading). The answer was on the same screen.

The roughly ₩1.38 trillion in foreign net selling on Nov. 16 reported in the Snowshagal Daily and market close page counts the KOSPI regular session only. The market coverage and timing differ, so the amounts differ.

Where do I find
the biggest sales?

The short answer
At the bottom of the same table. Click the sort arrow on the net purchases (value) column to put the smallest values first, and the biggest net sales rise to the top.
To follow one stock day by day, use 투자자별 거래실적(개별종목) in the same menu.
03Watch out

When the Numbers Change,
and Common Mistakes

Run the same query on the same day at 4 p.m. and again at 9 p.m., and you may get different numbers. The notice at the bottom of the screen explains why.

  1. 3:30 p.m.

    The regular session ends and the day's closing price is set.

  2. 3:40 p.m.

    The after-hours closing-price sessionTrading at the day's closing price only. The Korea Exchange runs it from 3:40 to 4 p.m. runs until 4 p.m.

  3. 3:45 p.m.

    Regular-session trades appear on the screen first (the notice says 'scheduled for 3:45 p.m.').

  4. 4 p.m.

    The Korea Exchange's after-marketA trading session held after the regular session ends. The Korea Exchange has run it from 4 to 8 p.m. since Sept. 14, 2026. Covered in Reading the Markets Part 18. session runs until 8 p.m.

  5. 8 p.m.

    Final figures, including after-hours trading, are released (the notice says 'scheduled for 8 p.m.').

How much can the numbers move? Here is the foreign investor column for Nov. 16, captured right after the close and checked again the next day.

SK Square3:51 p.m. +₩26.0 billion → final +₩27.4 billion
SK hynix3:51 p.m. −₩224.2 billion → final −₩204.2 billion
Samsung Electronics3:51 p.m. −₩619.4 billion → final −₩621.0 billion

This is one reason flow figures in stories published right after the close don't match the next morning's. The day's official closing price is still the 3:30 p.m. price.

Common MistakesFour to avoid
Mistake 01
"The table that first appears shows foreign net buying"
The screen opens with the investor type set to financial investment. You have to choose foreigners, institutions (total) or individuals yourself.
Mistake 02
"The top stock shows foreigners were buying today"
The top row is only the stock they bought most. Whether they bought or sold overall, you can tell only by scrolling to the bottom. On Nov. 16 the top row was +₩27.4 billion, yet across the three markets foreigners were net sellers of more than ₩1 trillion.
Mistake 03
"Reading a one-month query as a single day"
A longer period gives the total for that period. For a single day, set the start and end dates to the same day.
Mistake 04
"Financial investment is the same as institutions"
Financial investment is one part of institutions. To match what Korean news calls 'institutions,' choose institutions (total).
