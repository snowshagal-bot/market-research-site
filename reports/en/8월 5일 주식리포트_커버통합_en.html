Glossary One-Liner
Sidecar
A mechanism that halts program trading quotes for 5 minutes during sharp fluctuations in index futures. On Aug 5, it was triggered at 9:24 AM as KOSPI 200 futures moved+5.12%(the 22nd occurrence for KOSPI this year, according to reports). No trigger for KOSDAQ today.
Daily Limit Up · Daily Limit Down
Prices that reached the daily maximum fluctuation limit (±30%). On Aug 5, there were 0 stocks at the daily limit up on the KOSPI and 11 on the KOSDAQ, with zero daily limit downs across both markets.
SOX
PHLX Semiconductor Sector Index (SOX). A bellwether for global semiconductor stocks
ISM PMI
The ISM business sentiment index. A reading above 50 indicates expansion, while below 50 signals contraction. Following the manufacturing data, the services sector report is due on the night of Aug 5.
QRA
The U.S. Treasury’s Quarterly Refunding Announcement (QRA). The total borrowing amount and the composition of short-term and long-term maturities are key drivers of long-term interest rates.
JOLTS
The U.S. Department of Labor’s Job Openings and Labor Turnover Survey (JOLTS). Job openings are used to gauge the temperature of penta-labor market conditions. June job openings came in at 7.359 million, missing expectations.
Lock-up period expiration.
The lifting of lock-up restrictions that prevent major shareholders and Institutional Investors from selling shares for a set period post-IPO. This expiration date may introduce selling pressure.
hedge
Reducing the risk of loss on held assets through offsetting trades. Buying cash equities while selling futures mitigates directional index risk.
Adjusted Gross Margin
Non-GAAP gross margin, excluding one-off costs. As the criteria differ from GAAP figures, comparisons between estimates and actuals must be made using consistent accounting standards.
Program Trading
Automated orders that buy or sell multiple stocks simultaneously. This is a major channel that significantly impacts indices and Investor Flows. Since it is included in the investor-specific flow data, it is not aggregated separately.
Arbitrage Trading · Non-Arbitrage Program Trading
Arbitrage trading exploits price discrepancies between spot and futures markets, while non-arbitrage program trading refers to other automated strategies (such as index tracking). Non-arbitrage is a primary channel for spot orders from Foreign Investors and Institutional Investors.
Basis
Futures price minus spot index. A positive spread indicates contango, while a negative spread indicates backwardation. On Aug 5, the spread shifted from -0.03 to +3.46.
Contango · Backwardation
Contango is the normal market structure where futures are priced higher than spot; backwardation is the inverse. One must analyze the underlying cause of the shift (whether driven by a spot decline or a futures rally) to interpret it as a market signal.
Day Session FX Close
The final traded exchange rate during the domestic FX market’s day session (09:00–15:30). This serves as the KRW/USD benchmark for this report.
Standard Market Exchange Rate
The reference exchange rate announced daily, calculated as the weighted average of interbank transaction rates. The JPY/KRW basis for this report
Market Breadth
The ratio of advancing stocks rather than the index itself. An indicator measuring the breadth of market participation.
