One-Line Glossary
Limit Up ⁠· Limit Down
Daily price limit ±30% · Aug. 14 KOSPI 3 limit-up · KOSDAQ 9 limit-up · no limit-down
CPI ⁠· PPI
CPI = consumer inflation · PPI = producer inflation · released monthly by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics · July data due Aug. 12 and Aug. 13
FOMC
U.S. Federal Open Market Committee · policy-rate setting meeting · July meeting held rates, with three dissents favoring a hike
LPR
China Loan Prime Rate · de facto benchmark lending rate announced by the PBOC on the 20th of each month
SOX
Philadelphia Semiconductor Index (SOX) · key gauge of global semiconductor stocks
ADR
Depositary receipts of foreign companies listed in the U.S. · local-market trading price
HBM
High Bandwidth Memory · stacked architecture widens data pathways · used in AI servers
S7
A thematic basket grouping seven large-cap semiconductor stocks, including Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix
Materials, Parts & Equipment
Materials, Parts & Equipment · suppliers of materials and equipment to major semiconductor producers
EPS
Earnings per share · net income ÷ shares outstanding · adjusted EPS excludes one-off items
Consensus ⁠· Guidance
Consensus = average of brokerage forecasts · guidance = company-provided outlook
Short Selling
Selling borrowed shares → bearish positioning · Aug. 14 KOSPI KRW1.54tn · 4.2% of KRX+NXT turnover
Flow Concentration
Share of the sum of foreign net-buy stocks accounted for by the top net-buy names · KRX data
Program Trading
Automated multi-stock orders · a channel for index and flow moves · already included in investor-level flow data
Arbitrage ⁠· Non-arbitrage
Arbitrage = exploits cash-futures price differences · non-arbitrage = other program trading such as index tracking
Cash–Futures Closing Gap
Cash closes 15:30 / futures 15:45 → not a same-time basis · excluded from directional signals
Onshore Daytime Close
Final traded USD/KRW rate in domestic FX market, 09:00–15:30
Base Exchange Rate
Published volume-weighted average of interbank exchange rates · used for JPY/KRW
Sector ⁠· Theme Performance
Sector = market-cap weighted · theme = simple average of constituents · when values conflict, sector value is the reference
Market-cap weighted
Sector indexes weight stocks in proportion to market cap · larger stocks exert more influence
Market Breadth
Share of advancing stocks · gauge of how broadly gains and losses are distributed
