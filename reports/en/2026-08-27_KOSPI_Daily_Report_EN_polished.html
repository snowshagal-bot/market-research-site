Three Catalysts Arrived in Sequence

① U.S. inflation
July : headline +0.2% MoM · +3.7% YoY · core +0.2% MoM · +3.3% YoY · real was essentially flat. Headline inflation came in somewhat above market expectations and remained too high to say inflation pressure had sufficiently eased · U.S. 10-year yield 4.664%, +2.5bp · Nasdaq -0.08%. Discount-rate pressure was already present before the Korean open · the U.S. regular session had not ended in strong risk-on mode.

② NVIDIA
Reported after the U.S. regular-session close. Revenue $96.22B · adjusted EPS $2.22 · Q3 revenue guidance $108B ±2% all beat consensus · data-center revenue $89.0B. The key takeaway was not the quarter already reported, but confirmation that demand remained durable into Q3 · a direct catalyst for strength in Korean semiconductors and KOSPI's +2.76% gap-up open.

③ Bank of Korea
Policy rate 2.75% → 3.00% · 25bp · second consecutive hike. The 2026 growth forecast was raised 2.6% → 3.3% · median 6-month policy-rate outlook 3.25%. Stronger growth is supportive for corporate earnings, but it also widens the scope for further tightening · stronger tightening expectations weighed on the morning session.

U.S. inflation · rate pressure remains unresolved. Headline +3.7% YoY · core +3.3% YoY · discount-rate pressure was present before the open.
NVIDIA · pushed back against demand concerns. Revenue · adjusted EPS · Q3 guidance all beat consensus · data center $89.0B · the assumption of zero China data-center compute revenue was also constructive.
Bank of Korea · a stronger growth outlook actually increased room for tightening. Policy rate 3.00% · growth 2.6% → 3.3% · median 6-month policy-rate outlook 3.25%.
The three catalysts pointed in different directions · inflation and rates weighed through the discount rate · NVIDIA supported semiconductor earnings expectations · Korea opened +2.76% between those forces → closed +1.53%.
We do not read today's +1.53% as a simple risk-on move · index +104p · advancer ratio 35.0% · NVIDIA's earnings lifted a narrow group of leaders, but did not erase discount-rate pressure across the broader market.
