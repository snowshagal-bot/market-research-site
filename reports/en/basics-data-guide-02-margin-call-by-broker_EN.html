Short on time?Three key takeaways
  • Look for three numbers in your broker's app: your collateral ratio, the line applied to your account, and the shortfall. They sit on the collateral-ratio screen.
  • If the account closes below the line, the shares are usually sold two business days later, in the opening call auctionThe period before the open when orders are collected and then matched at a single price at 9 a.m. On the Korea Exchange it runs from 8:30 to 9 a.m.. How late you can still stop it that morning differs by broker.
  • The line is usually 140%. Since Nov. 9, 2026, some brokers have cut it to 130% while others only postpone a sale if you apply. The same ratio can lead to different outcomes at different brokers.

01The route

Finding Your Numbers

Friday evening, a text arrives from your broker: “Your collateral is short. Please add more.” That is a margin call. There is a screen to open at that point, and only three numbers to read on it. Part 20 of Market Basics explains what a forced saleWhen shares bought with borrowed money no longer cover the loan, or a payment is missed, the broker sells them without asking the investor. Called 반대매매 in Korean. is.

02How to read it

From the Text Message
to the Sale

A text does not mean an immediate sale. It does not mean plenty of time, either. Take a guess first.

Warm-upThink it through

Your account closes below its line on Friday, and a margin call arrives that evening. If you do nothing, when are the shares usually sold?

The answer is ②

Call the day of the shortfall D. You have until the next business day (D+1) to top up, and the shares are sold on the morning of the business day after that (D+2). Weekends and holidays are not counted. In effect, Monday is the only day left to act.

We say “usually” for a reason. At Kiwoom the deadline is a day longer for some account types. Some disclosures also say that in a sharp price move a broker may sell without a top-up demand (Korea Investment, Shinhan).

How late is too late that morning?

  1. 8:30 a.m.

    The Korea Exchange call auction begins. Forced-sale orders usually go in at this point.

  2. 9:00 a.m.

    The collected orders are filled at the opening priceThe first price of the day. On the Korea Exchange it is set by matching the call-auction orders at 9 a.m.. If there are too few buy orders, not everything may be sold that day.

Some brokers still leave a way out that morning: repay in full before the order goes out, or apply for one of the temporary postponements. The cutoffs differ.

Mirae AssetRepay in full by 8:50 a.m. and the order is cancelled automatically
KiwoomFor now, postponement requests 8 to 8:50 a.m.
Korea InvestmentFor now, postponement requests 8 to 8:30 a.m.
NHFor now, apply for the lowered line by 8:30 a.m.

These times come from news reports of broker notices in October and November 2026. The cutoff for a deposit is not the cutoff for a postponement request. For a broker not listed, ask the day before, and after a deposit check that the order was cancelled.

Does a forced sale
take everything?

In one sentence
The broker sells enough to bring the account back to the line, not just enough to cover the shortfall, so far more goes and it can be everything.
Selling shares cuts the loan, but it also cuts the collateral. Selling ₩200,000 of stock does not close a ₩200,000 gap.

Above 130%, so why did a text arrive?

The news says 130%, yet your account at 135% got a text. The reason is usually one of three.

A different stockAdministrative issuesStocks the exchange flags as close to meeting delisting criteria. carry 160%. At Samsung, stocks with a 50% initial margin rate carry 150%, so even a 10-point cut leaves them at 140%.
Mixed loansMirae Asset averages by loan size when an account also has a loan against overseas stocks (140%). With ₩4 million domestic and ₩6 million overseas, the line is 136%.
The line hasn't movedAt brokers that postpone, the line stays at 140% whether or not you apply, so the text still comes; only the sale day moves. NH also keeps 140% for accounts that did not consent in advance.

How long do these rules last?

  1. Jan. 31

    Last day of the regulator's waiver. It is a Sunday.

  2. Feb. 1

    Monday. It could be the first business day on which lowered lines go back up.

We have not found a broker that says when it will restore its line. KB says only “until further notice.” If the line returns to 140%, the 135% account above is ₩500,000 short again, even with prices unchanged.

Common MistakesFour to avoid
Mistake 01
“The news said 130%, so my account is at 130% too.”
Even within one broker it differs by stock, loan type and whether you applied. Read the line shown for your account.
Mistake 02
“It went above 140% during the day, so I'm fine today.”
The judgment uses the closing price. A level touched briefly during the session does not count.
Mistake 03
“I just need to deposit by 8:50 in the morning.”
Some brokers stop taking requests at 8:30 a.m., and the cutoff for a deposit is not the cutoff for a postponement request.
Mistake 04
“I'm ₩500,000 short, so ₩500,000 of stock gets sold.”
The broker sells enough to regain the line. That can be several times the shortfall, or everything.

MoreFinding notices, restoring collateral, unsettled purchases, KSFC

Finding notices · Search the notices in the broker's app or website for 담보유지비율 (maintenance ratio) or 반대매매 (forced sale). Kiwoom's November 2026 notice was titled 「신용/대출 담보부족 반대매매 임시 유예 안내」.

Ways to restore collateral · There are three. Deposit cash to cover the shortfall. Sell part of the margined shares yourself to cut the loan. Pledge other shares as collateral. Proceeds from your own sale come off the loan at settlement, two business days later, so check the screen again to see that the shortfall has cleared. Pledged shares count at only part of their market value.

Unsettled purchases run on a different clock · A margin loan is counted from the day collateral falls short. An unsettled purchase (미수, misu) is a balance left unpaid at settlement after buying with partial payment, so it is counted from the purchase date. If it is not paid by settlement, two business days later, the shares are sold the day after settlement (Mirae Asset disclosure). The collateral-ratio waiver does not change this.

Korea Securities Finance · A KSFC-funded loan is a margin loan a broker funds with money borrowed from Korea Securities Finance (KSFC). Whichever money is used, the maintenance ratio and sale dates follow your broker's disclosure.

The line below which the broker demands more collateral (담보유지비율)

Notes on the Numbers

Date checked · The cards and screen names were checked on Nov. 24, 2026, against each broker's public disclosures, help pages and guide pages. For the “for now” boxes we could not open every broker's original notice, so we also relied on news reports of notices issued Nov. 6–12.

The duty that was lifted · The 140% in the regulation is the collateral a broker must secure when it makes a margin loan. The line to be kept afterward, the maintenance ratio, is set by each broker, and the broker must demand more collateral as soon as an account falls below it. The Financial Supervisory Service lifted that duty from Nov. 9, 2026, to Jan. 31, 2027. Forced sales for unsettled purchases not paid by settlement are unchanged.

Morning cutoffs · Most disclosures do not say how late on the sale morning a deposit still cancels the order. One October 2026 report said most large brokers cancel automatically until 8:50 a.m., but we could not confirm that broker by broker. Kiwoom's help page says that, for unsettled-purchase sales only, deposits received by 8:55 a.m. are reflected. Samsung's disclosure for loans against securities (reviewed February 2025) says cash repayments on the sale day are processed at about 6:30, 8:15, 8:40 and 8:50 a.m.; we could not confirm that the same holds for margin loans today.

Kiwoom · The downloadable disclosure is the Jan. 24, 2025, revision. It gives the deadline as within the next business day after the first demand for Kiwoom-type accounts, and within the second business day for basic-type accounts.

Mirae Asset · When borrowing exceeds ₩3 billion, 10 percentage points are added to the line, and 20 points above ₩5 billion. The sizing price is 15% below the prior close for stock groups A–C and 20% below for groups D–F. Under the lowered line, news reports say a forced sale is sized to restore 130%.

Samsung · The key-facts sheet, the app guide and the website's margin and loan guide all give 150% for stocks with a 50% initial margin rate. When an account borrows against several stocks, its line is an average weighted by loan size. The sizing price is 85% of the prior close for grades S and A, and 80% for grade B and below. News reports say the line was lowered by 10 points without application on Nov. 9 only, and by application after that.

Korea Investment · The Nov. 9, 2026, disclosure states only 140%, while the key-facts sheet on its website says 140–160%. It values collateral at about 3:40 p.m. and 8:10 p.m. and uses the Korea Exchange close even when Nextrade prices differ. In another example in the same disclosure, all 1,000 shares of an account that borrowed ₩5 million are sold and ₩500,000 of debt still remains. News reports say online requests were not opened to accounts set up at a branch.

NH · We could not open the margin trading disclosure. Consent is not accepted from 3:30 to 4:30 p.m. For overseas-stock loans, accounts that consented get 160%.

KB · The first report, on Nov. 6, said the lowered line would run until Dec. 11. Every later report says until further notice. The loan disclosure gives the sizing price as the prior close × 70%.

Shinhan · The sizing price is 15% below the prior close for grades A, B and C, and 20% below for grades D, E and Z. News reports say it extended relief to overseas stocks, but we could not confirm what line it uses for overseas-stock collateral (usually 170%). Its guide says rollover terms can differ between loans funded by the broker itself and KSFC-funded loans.

Initial margin rate · In September 2026 the Korea Financial Investment Association proposed raising the minimum from 45% to 50%, and the start date differs by broker: Nov. 2 at KB, Nov. 9 at Korea Investment, Nov. 20 at Kiwoom and Dec. 1 at Hanwha, according to news reports.

Korea Securities Finance · Kiwoom shows KSFC-funded balances on screen [0901]. KSFC also lends directly to individuals against stocks, bonds and funds; those terms are in the agreement with KSFC, not with a broker.

Unsettled purchases · An unsettled purchase above ₩100,000 means 30 days of cash-only buying at every broker.

Other brokers · Daishin used 140%, 150% or 160% by stock group and cut each by 10 points; Hana cut by grade. Meritz postpones by one day for accounts at 120% or above, according to news reports.

Made-up numbers · The 135% account that borrowed ₩10 million and the Nov. 27 calendar are examples created for explanation. We assumed the shares count as collateral at full market value and left out costs.
