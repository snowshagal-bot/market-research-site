iM Securities analyst Yang Hyun-kyung cited concerns about financing and profitability of AI infrastructure investment, China's CXMT catching up technologically, and pressure from foreign selling as factors behind the October 13 decline.12 In the preceding US session, the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell by about 2.6% and Micron by roughly 3.9%.8, 13

CXMT · Year-end product launch planned; mass-production viability unverified

At an October 10 event, CXMT's president said the company would introduce server DDR5 using a 4F² cell architecture by year-end. A Morningstar analyst, cited by Bloomberg, expects a substantial gap between unveiling a product and commercializing it at economically viable yields. Korean industry sources say Samsung Electronics and SK hynix also possess 4F² technology but do not use it for volume production because of yield and cost constraints.14, 15, 16

The statement came on October 10. An October 12 closing-market report also cited the possibility that Chinese memory makers could gain share, but did not establish whether it referred to the same development.7, 14

Developments discussed on October 13 should not be retroactively presented as explanations for the preceding two-session selloff.

THE SHORT ANSWER

  1. Earnings did not fall. Q3 operating profit was up 20.0% even from the previous quarter, and Hana Securities' KRW 116tn Q4 forecast is about 8% higher again. Yet over the two sessions following the release, the KOSPI fell 6.2%, Samsung Electronics 6.7%, and SK hynix 6.8%, all measured from the October 7 close.CALCULATED3, 4, 5, 7, 17
  2. Against expectations, the surprise was modest. The result slightly exceeded some pre-release estimates of KRW 105–106tn, but missed FnGuide's October 1 consensus of KRW 110.2803tn. Hana Securities notes that the Q3 estimate had already been revised down from a peak of KRW 112tn toward KRW 106tn.2, 3, 5, 6
  3. Slower growth was already in the forecasts. Before the announcement, I/B/E/S data dated October 6 projected Korea EPS growth of 36.0% in 2027 and 4.8% in 2028. Subsequent Eugene and Hana reports pointed in the same direction.1, 2, 3
  4. The direct cause of the two-day drop cannot be isolated. SK hynix fell alongside Samsung even though its profit estimates had barely moved. Foreign selling, elevated US long-term yields, questions about AI-company revenue, and Chinese memory competitors were all cited.7, 18
  5. The next test is 2027–2028 estimates. Around November 5, the first scheduled checkpoint (calculated), compare 2027 operating-profit consensus against its October 7 baseline—KRW 1,330tn for the KOSPI, as cited by Kiwoom—using the same data provider and fiscal-year definitions wherever possible.5

Samsung Electronics announced preliminary Q3 operating profit of KRW 107.4tn on the morning of October 8. Over the following two trading sessions, its shares fell 6.7% and SK hynix fell 6.8%, measured from the October 7 close.CALCULATED4, 7, 17 The broader decline, however, began before the earnings release. The KOSPI had fallen for four consecutive sessions from October 6 to 12, shedding 8.85%; on October 7, the day before the announcement, it was already down 1.98%.5, 19

Semiconductors account for 56.7% of KOSPI market capitalization. Sector net-income growth is forecast to slow from 592.9% in 2026 to 37.5% in 2027.3 The slowdown is not confined to chips. Eugene forecasts KOSPI operating-profit growth excluding semiconductors at 39% in 2026 and 12% in 2027.1

Korea is not the only market where earnings growth is forecast to slow. Hana's Bloomberg data show S&P 500 EPS growth declining from 35% in 2026 to 16% in 2027 (34.2% to 17.2% in I/B/E/S), even as the S&P 500 continued making new highs during this period, according to iM Securities' Oct 12 report.2, 3, 24 The bigger divergence comes in 2028. Global growth at 14.1%, the S&P 500 at 17.2%, and Japan at 10.4% remain close to their 2027 levels; Korea drops from 36.0% to 4.8%.2

Daishin Securities argued in its Oct 12 weekly report that the market was discounting the durability of earnings from 2027 onward (as reported by Aju Business Daily).18 iM Securities similarly argues that SK hynix will need evidence that profits can endure beyond 2028 before investors reduce the discount applied to potential peak-cycle earnings. It attributes the recent widening of that discount, however, to Korea-specific factors such as foreign selling and reports of a possible Solidigm listing.24

TAKEAWAY
  • It is the growth rate that slows; earnings levels are still projected to rise (based on the two datasets with 2028 estimates).
  • Korea has the sharpest 2027→2028 slowdown in the I/B/E/S table. The US also decelerates from 2026→2027, while the S&P 500 has been reaching new highs.
4Counterarguments

What the earnings trajectory cannot explain

To attribute the two-session fall solely to a slowdown that was known before the announcement, we would need separate evidence that expectations deteriorated further after the release.

COUNTERPOINT 1

A stock with steady estimates fell, too

SK hynix's 2026 operating-profit consensus had slipped just 0.67% over the preceding month, according to FnGuide.18 Nevertheless, its shares fell 6.8% over two sessions, much like Samsung's.CALCULATED7, 17

One-month changes in 2026 consensus estimates are not enough to explain the two stocks' simultaneous decline. If prices were newly reflecting 2027–2028 profitability or a higher risk premium, this comparison would not capture it. Nor do unchanged analyst estimates necessarily mean investors' expectations have remained unchanged.

COUNTERPOINT 2

Foreign flows, rates, AI and China

Yuanta Securities analyst Lee Jae-won called foreign investor flows, rather than semiconductor earnings, the domestic market's biggest immediate concern. Foreign investors net sold KRW 2.0186tn of KOSPI stocks on October 12, including more than KRW 1tn of SK hynix.7, 19 Net selling is an observed market outcome. These data cannot tell us whether it was an independent cause or the mechanism through which earnings and interest-rate concerns were reflected in prices.

The US 10-year Treasury yield peaked at 5.31% in the October daily series on Oct 5, then edged down to 5.28% on Oct 7 and 5.24% on Oct 9. It remained elevated throughout the period (US Treasury daily closing data).25 The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index was down 4.3% for the week in iM Securities' Oct 12 report.24 An October 8 Financial Times report put OpenAI's annualized revenue run rate at roughly $50bn as of late September, prompting questions about a previously reported figure near $70bn. Bloomberg's $70bn figure the following day was not a current run rate but the company's projected year-end 2026 annualized revenue, as presented to investors. Hana's quant team saw the dispute as one of revenue-recognition conventions rather than a clear change in underlying AI demand.21, 22, 26 Reports on October 12 also raised the prospect of Chinese memory suppliers gaining market share.7

COUNTERPOINT 3

Current operating conditions, seen through exports

Slower forecast earnings growth need not mean deteriorating operations. September exports reached a monthly record $120.94bn, up 83.5% year on year and above June's previous record. Semiconductors drove the gain at $60.3bn (+262.8%), but exports outside chips also rose 23% (as reported from the trade ministry's release).27, 28 Exports over October 1–10 increased 102.0% in total value, partly reflecting more working days; the average per working day rose 28.5% (Korea Customs Service).29 Export figures are nominal values combining prices and volumes, not measures of corporate earnings growth.

At Micron's late-September earnings call, CEO Sanjay Mehrotra said fiscal 2027 should be stronger than fiscal 2026 and that memory and storage supply-demand conditions in calendar 2027–2028 should be much tighter than in 2026.30 That is management's outlook. Tight supply can persist while growth rates slow against an already enlarged earnings base. The comments therefore do not necessarily contradict the forecast slowdown in Korean EPS growth.

COUNTERPOINT 4

A 5.1× forward P/E

Daishin Securities wrote in its Oct 12 weekly report that the KOSPI's 12-month forward P/E (current price divided by expected EPS over the next 12 months) had fallen to 5.1×, with no underlying price date specified. It judged that prices already reflected considerable concern about the semiconductor cycle peaking and earnings deteriorating.18

Holding the forward EPS figure of 1,293.5 index points from Daishin's Oct 8 report constant, a 6× P/E implies a KOSPI level of 7,761 (the framework discussed in NO.07); applying the same fixed EPS to the Oct 12 close yields 4.9×. Both are mechanical calculations using an earlier EPS estimate; any fair-value multiple requires separate assumptions.CALCULATED2

What this note could—and could not—establish
ESTABLISHED: Against the different benchmarks, KRW 107.4tn ranged from a +2.3% beat to a −4.1% miss—not a large surprise relative to expectations. NOT ESTABLISHED: These data cannot show whether the already anticipated slowdown directly caused the two-session decline, or how much of the move each factor explains. The fact that the index started falling before the announcement makes isolating the earnings reaction harder still.

6Decision criteria

Three figures to check by early November

The question now is whether 2027–2028 forecasts will be revised upward from earlier expectations—and whether share prices respond if they are.

  1. 2027 operating-profit consensus versus the Oct 7 baseline

    The baseline is KRW 1,330tn for KOSPI companies, KRW 554tn for Samsung Electronics, and KRW 401tn for SK hynix (cited by Kiwoom Securities; data aggregator unspecified).5 The first checkpoint is around November 5, four weeks after the release (calculated). As context, Hana's historical 2014–2025 four-week averages show next-year estimates rising 0.4% for a "beat amid downward revisions" and 7.6% for beats overall (sample sizes undisclosed).3

  2. SK hynix Q3 operating profit versus its final pre-release consensus

    FnGuide's October 1 figure was KRW 78.0577tn.6 Keep that as an earlier benchmark, then obtain the same provider's immediately pre-release estimate for the actual comparison. Hyundai Motor Securities argues that the key test is whether Q3 results provide evidence of a memory shortage continuing through 2028.31

  3. 2028 EPS estimates · I/B/E/S Korea growth baseline: 4.8%

    Check whether 2028 estimates rise alongside 2027 estimates. If the 2028 growth rate rises only because the 2027 base has been cut, that does not count as an improvement.2

COMPARISON RULE Compare the same data provider, constituent universe, fiscal year, and earnings measure wherever possible. If estimates rise but stocks do not follow, revisit pricing factors outside earnings forecasts, including flows and interest rates.

Glossary
EPS · Earnings per share
Net income divided by the number of shares. Growth rates here are year-on-year.
Consensus · Expectations · Estimates
Consensus is the average of multiple analysts' forecasts. In this note, "expectations" generally denotes the immediately pre-release quarterly consensus; "estimates" refers more broadly to quarterly or annual earnings projections.
I/B/E/S · FnGuide · Quantiwise
Data aggregators that compile analysts' forecasts. Their coverage and definitions differ, so the same market can have different estimates.
12-month forward P/E
Current share price divided by expected earnings per share over the next 12 months.
Preliminary vs final earnings
A company's initial summary earnings announcement versus its later, more detailed release including segment results.
Return to profitability
A move from a loss to a profit. With a loss in the comparison year, a conventional YoY growth rate is undefined, so the following year's rate cannot be compared as an acceleration.
LPDDR
Low-power DRAM used in mobile devices and servers, among other applications.
