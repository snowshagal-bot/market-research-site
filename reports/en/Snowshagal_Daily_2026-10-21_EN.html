WHY / 02

Post-Close U.S. AI Results and a Tripling of Chip Exports Fueled a Narrow Rally in Semiconductor Heavyweights

What changed in just one day?

IN PLAIN ENGLISHAfter the U.S. closing bell, SpaceX posted third-quarter results far above expectations, while AI server maker Super Micro Computer reported preliminary revenue above forecasts. That eased some of the worries about AI investment payback that had dragged the KOSPI lower a day earlier, helping Samsung Electronics and SK hynix gain more than 5%. Reports also pointed to a tripling of semiconductor exports and some relief from oil and bond yields. But the rebound was concentrated in the biggest chip stocks.

CATALYST / 06

Align Puts SK’s Discount to NAV at 75%; Shares Jump 12%

One company-specific development that the market priced in today
SK Inc. · Align's remarks on Oct 20, U.S. local time
+12.62%

Activist manager Align Partners disclosed a 1.5% stake in SK and argued that its shares trade at an approximately 75% discount to net asset value.

WHAT · The announcement

Align Partners Asset Management CEO Lee Chang-hwan said at the 13D Active-Passive Investor Summit in the U.S. on October 20 that the fund holds 1.5% of SK Inc. The stake was described in remarks at the event, not in a regulatory filing (EToday).

TERMS · The valuation case

Align argues that SK hynix's value is discounted twice, first at SK Square and again at SK Inc. The roughly 1x P/E ratio is based on Align's own sum of earnings attributable to SK through its effective holdings in individual subsidiaries.

ASK · What Align wants

Citing SK Square's discount targets and shareholder-return record as a benchmark, Align argued that SK Inc.'s new value-enhancement plan, expected before year-end, should include an explicit NAV-discount target and even stronger shareholder returns. Commentary also suggested that changes in directors' fiduciary duties could become a means of pressuring the board (Bloter).

SHAREHOLDERS · What it could mean (interpretation)

The share-price move reflects expectations that an activist stake disclosure and specific demands may put pressure on SK's year-end value-up plan. A 1.5% stake is too small to influence management on its own. The next test is whether SK's new plan includes a discount target and shareholder-return measures.

MARKET · The reaction

SK Inc. closed at ₩482,000 (+12.62%); by 10:54 a.m., its gain had already reached +11.21% (reported). SK Square's gain was +5.13%, roughly in line with SK hynix (+5.81%). Other holding companies diverged: CJ rose, while LG and Hanwha fell (Pinpoint News, 3:22 p.m.).
