One-Line Glossary
sidecar
If KOSPI 200 futures stay at least 5% above or below the reference price for one minute, program-trading orders are suspended for five minutes · triggered on the buy side at 09:57 on Aug. 20
Limit Up· Limit Down
Daily price limit ±30% · Aug. 20: KOSPI 4 limit-ups · KOSDAQ 5 limit-ups · KOSDAQ 1 limit-down
FOMC
Federal Open Market Committee · policy-rate-setting meeting · July meeting held 3.50–3.75%, with three dissenters favoring a hike
Treasury buybacks
Treasury debt-management tool that repurchases outstanding securities · this expansion supports liquidity in 10–30Y maturities · not the same as reducing net debt or Federal Reserve QE
CMT
Official Treasury maturity yields based on 15:30 ET quote collection · not actual final transaction prices
LPR
China Loan Prime Rate · the de facto benchmark lending rate published by the PBOC on the 20th of each month
SOX
Philadelphia Semiconductor Index (SOX) · key gauge of global semiconductor stocks
ADR
Depositary receipt for a foreign company listed in the U.S. · locally traded price
S7
A thematic basket grouping seven large-cap semiconductor stocks, including Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix
HBM
High Bandwidth Memory · DRAM stacked in multiple layers to increase bandwidth
Materials, Parts & Equipment
Materials, Parts & Equipment · suppliers of materials and equipment to major semiconductor producers
EPS
Earnings per share · net income ÷ shares outstanding · adjusted EPS excludes one-off items
Consensus· Guidance
Consensus = average of brokerage forecasts · guidance = company-provided outlook
Short Selling
Selling borrowed shares → bearish positioning · Aug. 20 KOSPI KRW1.97tn · 5.1% of combined KRX+NXT turnover
Flow Concentration
Share of each side's aggregate stock-level buys/sells accounted for by the top names · KRX data
Program Trading
Automated multi-stock orders · a channel for index and flow moves · already included in investor-level flow data
Arbitrage· Non-arbitrage
Arbitrage = exploits cash-futures price differences · non-arbitrage = other program trading such as index tracking
Cash–Futures Basis
Cash closes 15:30 / futures 15:45 → not the same timestamp, so excluded from directional signals · positive basis = contango
Base Exchange Rate
Volume-weighted average of interbank FX transactions · market-average-rate series · differs from the final regular-session transaction rate · USD/KRWreference is the 15:30 domestic FX daytime-session close
Sector· Theme Performance
Sector = market-cap weighted · theme = simple average of constituents · when values conflict, sector value is the reference
Market-cap weighted
Sector indexes weight stocks in proportion to market cap · larger stocks exert more influence
Market Breadth
Share of advancing stocks · gauge of how broadly gains and losses are distributed
