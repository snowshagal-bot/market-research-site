Glossary One-Liner
Sidecar
A mechanism that halts Program Trading quotes for 5 minutes during sharp fluctuations in index futures. On Aug 6, the sell-sidecar was triggered at 10:18 AM as Mini KOSPI 200 futures fell-5.04%(the 46th time for KOSPI this year, and the 24th time on the sell side, according to reports). No sidecar was triggered for KOSDAQ today.
Daily Limit Up · Daily Limit Down
Prices that reached the daily maximum fluctuation limit (±30%). On Aug 6, there were 3 stocks at Daily Limit Up on the KOSPI and 7 on the KOSDAQ; there were 0 stocks at Daily Limit Down across both markets.
SOX
PHLX Semiconductor Sector Index (SOX). A bellwether for global semiconductor stocks
ISM Index
The Institute for Supply Management’s sentiment index for the economy. Readings above 50 indicate expansion, while those below indicate contraction. The July services index came in at 54.1, missing expectations.
QRA
The U.S. Treasury’s quarterly refunding announcement. The total borrowing amount and the composition of short-term and long-term maturities drive long-term interest rates. The August announcement confirmed $125.0bn with no increase in issuance.
Storage (NAND · HDD)
Devices used for data storage. SanDisk specializes in NAND flash, while Western Digital handles hard disk drives (HDD). The two companies separated from a single firm in February 2025 and are both capturing AI data center demand.
SanDisk · WDC
SanDisk produces NAND flash, and Western Digital (WDC) manufactures hard disk drives. Both companies released their earnings early on Aug 6, and both shares fell in after-hours trading.
Consensus · Company Earnings Guidance
Consensus represents the average of brokerage estimates, while company earnings guidance reflects the outlook provided directly by the firm for the upcoming quarter. Share prices on earnings release dates often react more significantly to guidance than to past performance.
After-hours trading
Trading that continues after the close of the regular session. As earnings are released post-market, the initial price reaction is captured here; however, thin trading volume can lead to exaggerated downside swings.
Gross margin
The ratio of profit after deducting the cost of goods sold from revenue. A higher value indicates a larger profit margin per unit sold.
HBM
High Bandwidth Memory (HBM). A type of DRAM that stacks multiple layers to facilitate high-speed data transmission; it is a core component for AI servers.
ADP Private Payrolls
The private payrolls indicator compiled by ADP. It is widely viewed as a precursor to the employment report released two days later.
Current Account
The net balance of transactions for goods, services, and income exchanged with foreign entities. Compiled monthly by the Bank of Korea, the June figure marked a record high surplus of $49.73bn.
Lock-up period expiration.
The lifting of lock-up restrictions that prevent major shareholders and Institutional Investors from selling shares for a set period post-IPO. This expiration date may introduce selling pressure.
Program Trading
Automated orders that buy or sell multiple stocks simultaneously. This is a major channel that significantly impacts indices and Investor Flows. Since it is included in the investor-specific flow data, it is not aggregated separately.
Arbitrage Trading · Non-Arbitrage Program Trading
Arbitrage trading exploits price discrepancies between spot and futures markets, while non-arbitrage program trading refers to other automated strategies (such as index tracking). Non-arbitrage is a primary channel for spot orders from Foreign Investors and Institutional Investors.
Basis
Futures price minus spot index. A positive value indicates contango, while a negative value indicates backwardation. On 8/6, the spread shifted from +3.46 back to -1.77.
Contango · Backwardation
Contango is the normal market structure where futures are priced higher than spot; backwardation is the inverse. One must analyze the underlying cause of the shift (whether driven by a spot decline or a futures rally) to interpret it as a market signal.
Day Session FX Close
The final traded exchange rate during the domestic FX market’s day session (09:00–15:30). This serves as the KRW/USD benchmark for this report.
Standard Market Exchange Rate
The reference exchange rate announced daily, calculated as the weighted average of interbank transaction rates. The JPY/KRW basis for this report
Market Breadth
The ratio of advancing stocks rather than the index itself. An indicator measuring the breadth of market participation.
