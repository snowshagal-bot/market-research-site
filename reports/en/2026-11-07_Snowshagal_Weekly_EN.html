THE CHARACTER OF THE SELLOFF

The market did not question AI demand so much as ·
the speed and financing of data-center construction.

Already in forceSince August, Texas has audited data-center projects awaiting grid-interconnection approval, withholding approval pending review · In September it also halted issuance of environmental permits tied to data centers.

The electionOn Nov. 3, Democrats took both chambers of Congress · A candidate pledging a moratorium on new data-center approvals won in Texas · Existing measures could last longer or tighten · The new governor takes office Jan. 19, 2027.

The regulatory mapFive states newly won by Democratic governors account for 41.2% of planned or under-construction U.S. data-center power capacity; Texas alone accounts for 26.9%, according to Eugene Investment & Securities.

Financing clockBig Tech's successive financing deals raised concerns over AI funding on Nov. 3 · Strong U.S. employment data renewed rate worries on Nov. 6 · IREN still aims to raise roughly $6bn of its remaining investment funding.

Construction clockConcerns grew after the election that data-center construction could be delayed · IREN, citing customer design changes and supplier schedules, postponed the go-live target for facilities capable of more than $4bn in from year-end to January 2027 (potentially late February).

What has NOT happenedThere is no confirmed fall in AI demand or cancellation of existing contracts · IREN also says the relevant facilities have secured customers and funding.

Korean spilloverRegulatory fears translated into concentrated foreign selling of SK Hynix · The circuit breaker was this year's 11th.

The next testWill CoreWeave's Nov. 11 earnings confirm that its investment plans remain intact? ·02 Q1
