WHY / 02

AI Funding Doubts, Exporter Earnings and a Solidigm Listing Dispute Overwhelmed the U.S. Chip Rebound

Why Korea fell while U.S. semiconductors rose

IN PLAIN ENGLISHU.S. chip stocks finally rose after eight consecutive declines, but gave back more than half their intraday gain. Questions about the cost of AI investment and the sustainability of semiconductor profits persisted as Treasury yields climbed. In Korea, the won was at its strongest in two years, while earnings expectations for exporters—including defense, shipbuilding and autos—had been marked down. SK hynix also faced uncertainty over a potential U.S. listing of its subsidiary.

Hyundai Rotem's Third-Quarter Operating Profit Falls 11% to ₩247.1bn

WHAT · THE REPORTED NUMBERS

Consolidated revenue was ₩1.6207tn (+0.1%), with operating profit of ₩247.1bn (-11%). The Defense, Robotics & Hydrogen division, which generates most of the company's profit, reported revenue of ₩841.2bn (-10.1%) and operating profit of ₩236bn (-10.5%). Rail revenue rose to ₩656.1bn (+21.4%). The stood at ₩29.142tn, down ₩1.2626tn from the end of Q2 (Aju Economy).

EXPECTATIONS · THE CONSENSUS COMPARISON

Operating profit missed market forecasts by 10.5%. Samsung Securities cut its share-price target from ₩250,000 to ₩156,000 to reflect delayed export orders and currency moves (Financial News). Shinhan Securities (₩230,000 → ₩150,000), iM Securities, SK Securities and Kiwoom Securities also cut their targets, citing delays in new overseas defense orders and lower earnings forecasts for 2027 (NewsTNT).

IMPACT · WHAT IT MEANS FOR THE BUSINESS (INTERPRETATION)

Total revenue was roughly flat year on year. An approximately ₩116bn increase in rail sales offset an estimated ₩95bn decrease in Defense, Robotics & Hydrogen sales and a decline in plant revenue (Snowshagal calculation from reported growth rates). The smaller backlog adds uncertainty over when earnings can recover without confirmed new export orders, weighing on the near-term share-price outlook.
