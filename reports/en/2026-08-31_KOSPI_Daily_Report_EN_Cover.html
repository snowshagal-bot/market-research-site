THE READ

The gap-down was Korea repricing weekend developments before much of the world had opened — more a question of sequencing than sheer magnitude. The low arrived in the first five-minute bar, showing that selling did not persist for long. Morning rebounds were pushed back twice; after 13:30 the direction became much more one-sided. The close was effectively the high, but this was still hard to call a broad market advance.
